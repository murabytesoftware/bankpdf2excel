# BankPDF2Excel

Turn PDF bank statements into Excel, CSV, QuickBooks Web Connect (QBO) for QuickBooks and Xero, and Tally XML - offline on your PC, with per-row confidence checks. Built for accountants at month end.

## What it does

- Converts common US, UK, Gulf and EU statement layouts, including debit/credit columns and decimal-comma amounts.
- Exports Excel (.xlsx), CSV, QuickBooks Web Connect (.qbo) and Tally XML.
- Per-row confidence flags and a side-by-side view help you check extracted lines against the original statement.
- Everything runs locally on your Windows PC - statement files are never uploaded for conversion.

## Review before importing

Compatibility depends on the bank layout and PDF quality. Verify dates, amounts, balances and transaction direction before relying on an export, and test the target accounting workflow before business use. If your bank's PDF format does not parse correctly, contact us and we will take a look.
